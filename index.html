Who's behind it

AO is one person. Eliza Wood, working from Woodbridge, Virginia, since 2019 — drawing, coding, writing and shipping all of it without a team or outside money.

The art comes first, but the whole thing is built by hand too: the site, the shop, the games. Sixteen years of moderating a large online community taught the rest — that a place is only as good as how people are treated in it.

Tater is a miniature pinscher and takes his security duties seriously. Clifford is an orange tabby and takes nothing seriously. Both appear regularly and neither has agreed to a contract.
