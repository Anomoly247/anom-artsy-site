What this is

One studio.
Many worlds.

AO is a creative-tech studio built by one person since 2019 — digital art, character design, digital identity goods, children's content, community software, and the brand systems that hold them together.

The parts are not separate ventures. Art made in one place becomes a digital item in another, a character in a third, a lesson in a fourth. What one division learns, the rest inherit.

The web is how we find and grow our identity. Every connection makes the next one easier to see.

That idea is why this page looks the way it does. The divisions below aren't a chart of departments — they're a map of things reaching toward each other.
